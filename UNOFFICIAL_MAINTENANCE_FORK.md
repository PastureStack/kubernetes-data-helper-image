# Unofficial Maintenance Fork

This repository is maintained by the PastureStack community for legacy 1.6 compatibility work.

PastureStack is an independent community maintenance project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the historical platform vendor or SUSE.

Third-party names are used only for necessary provenance and compatibility identification. Keep the catalog and runtime contract described in [COMPATIBILITY.md](COMPATIBILITY.md) intact unless a migration plan explicitly documents the compatibility break and rollback path.
